**Infrastructure (21–28):** 21 buy GPU Server, 22 buy GPU Cluster, 23 buy Supercomputer, 24 sell for 50%, 25 daily upkeep, 26 compute speeds training, 27 one training slot per machine, 28 compute/slot/speed summary.

**Benchmarks (57–63):** 57–61 five suites (Reasoning, Coding, Math, Safety, Multilingual) with family fit, 62 leaderboard vs top competitor with state-of-the-art reputation bonus, 63 run history.

**Comparison (64–66):** 64 table of all models, 65 sortable columns, 66 best model highlighted.

**Conferences (75–78):** 75 generated conferences, 76 attend (+5 rep), 77 present a paper (20 RP, +12 rep, hype), 78 recruit candidates there.

**Government (79–84):** 79 six generated bills, 80 lobby for, 81 lobby against, 82 votes on support %, 83 passed bills add compliance costs and reputation changes, 84 research grant with 90-day cooldown.

**Economy (85–86):** 85 20% corporate tax on profit, 86 real Debt and Taxes figures in Economy.

**Events (87–96):** 87 viral demo, 88 data breach, 89 GPU shortage, 90 talent poaching, 91 lawsuit, 92 press praise, 93 server outage, 94 government contract, 95 breakthrough paper, 96 market crash (all logged on News & Events).

**Achievements (97–100):** 97 ten achievements (first model, first release, $1M, $10M, score 90+, #1 rank, 10 models, 5 staff, all research, 365 days), 98 unlock toasts, 99 progress counter, 100 locked/unlocked cards.

**Market & risk (101–106, added later):** 101 context window and safety tuning options with plain-English hints on every model option, 102 daily per-model demand with viral spikes and slumps, 103 newer released versions halve older ones' income, 104 market boom/bust cycles (top-bar badge, logged on News), 105 safety incidents for models with no or standard safety tuning, 106 rival pressure: income up to +20% when you lead the best competitor, down to -30% when you trail.
